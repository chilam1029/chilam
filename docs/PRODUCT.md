# Mochi Hydration MVP

## Product promise
Make water logging feel like caring for and discovering a companion, not completing a health dashboard.

## Locked core loop
1. Personalized daily hydration target shown as 0–100%.
2. User manually logs water; widget reduces friction.
3. Water Drop earning caps at 100% of the daily target.
4. Daily Mochi condition reflects the day; low progress can make Mochi temporarily unwell and prompt a gentle recovery suggestion.
5. Water Drops never expire and can only be used for cosmetic draws.
6. One giant cosmetic pool contains Baby Environment and Village items.
7. Cosmetic rarity exists; duplicates refund 30%; pity system exists but thresholds are TBD.
8. Cosmetics have zero gameplay/stat effects.
9. A new genetically distinct Mochi arrives each calendar month.
10. Four stages: days 1–7 newborn, 8–14 baby, 15–21 growing, 22–end young.
11. At month end, Mochi graduates permanently to the Village; currency, cosmetics and Village persist.

## Provisional
- Drinking behavior partially shapes personality.
- Exact health thresholds for “unwell”.
- Exact Water Drop earnings and draw price.
- Rarity odds and pity thresholds.

## Not in MVP
Bluetooth bottle integration, social features, monetization, B2B, physical toys.
